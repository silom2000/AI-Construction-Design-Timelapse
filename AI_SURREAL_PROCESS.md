# Surreal Transformation Timelapse — Process Document

## Core Aesthetic: Ethereal Brutalism

You are generating a **Surreal Transformation Timelapse** — not a construction timelapse.
The subject undergoes a physical metamorphosis across 6 anchor states.
The world obeys **hyperrealistic physics** (correct shadows, reflections, refraction, weight),
but the **object itself** defies material logic.

### Visual Language
- **Textures**: matte concrete, raw titanium, frosted glass, obsidian, liquid mercury, crystalline lattices
- **Palette**: desaturated, "expensive" — no saturated neons, no psychedelic colors
- **Light**: single dominant source, physically accurate cast shadows and reflections at all times
- **Feel**: photorealistic enough to be mistaken for real footage at first glance

### Forbidden
- Bright rainbow palettes or acid colors
- Cartoon or painterly rendering
- Cheap CGI glow effects
- Multiple objects appearing mid-sequence without physical cause

---

## The 6 Transformation Stages

Each stage is a frozen photographic moment — one physical state of the object.
The transformation logic must feel like a time-lapse of an impossible but internally consistent process.

| Stage | Name | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| 1 | ORIGIN STATE | The object as it exists before transformation — raw, heavy, grounded |
| 2 | FIRST SHIFT | Subtle material change begins — surface texture mutates, hairline fractures or crystallization appears |
| 3 | EMERGENCE | Impossible element breaks through — liquid glass veins, titanium ribs, floating shards orbit the mass |
| 4 | STRUCTURAL RUPTURE | Major physical change — gravity-defying portion, impossible geometry, phantom interior revealed |
| 5 | NEAR COMPLETE | New form dominates — the original material is almost consumed, final shape is clear |
| 6 | FINAL REVELATION | Complete transformation — cinematic orbital reveal of the finished impossible object |

---

## Camera Rules

- **Stages 1–5**: locked tripod, zero camera movement, static background — only the object transforms
- **Stage 6**: smooth cinematic drone arc, slow orbital reveal around the completed object
- **Angle**: close elevated 30-degree oblique — object fills 65–80% of the 9:16 frame
- Never: top-down, nadir, flat orthographic, distant aerial

---

## Audio Identity

**Surreal Atmospheric SOUND DESIGN** — no construction machinery.
Use: deep sub-bass resonance, crystalline high-frequency tones, slow glass harmonic drones,
metallic tension creaks, distant wind through impossible geometry, low tectonic rumble.
Audio must feel like the sound of matter rearranging itself.
